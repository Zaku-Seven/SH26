PUZZLE 1: WAKE JIB

Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, or Firefox.

Controls
- Move: Arrow keys or WASD
- Interact: Z, Enter, or Space
- Reset level: R

Sequence
1. Walk to the red house and interact with the door.
2. Check Jib on the couch.
3. Search the refrigerator for the energy drink.
4. Return to Jib and wake him.
5. Receive the quest to find Nick at the Rec Center.
6. Leave through the bottom doorway to finish Puzzle 1.

The game renders internally at the Game Boy Advance resolution of 240x160.
